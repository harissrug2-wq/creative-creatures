01. Overview & Scope

At Creative Creatures, we respect the privacy of agency owners, leadership teams, and partners who trust us with their agency operational and diagnostic data. We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality, security, and integrity of all data processed through our platform.

This Privacy Policy applies to all services offered by Creative Creatures, including our website located at CreativeCreatures.co / CreativeCreatures.org, our Owner Archetype assessment, Agency Diagnostic platform, Breakthrough Accelerator, and integrated back-office analytics dashboards.

Key Privacy Promise We never sell, rent, or trade your agency's operational data, client lists, or financial metrics to third parties or advertising networks. Your agency performance data is strictly yours.

02. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, personalize, and improve our agency diagnostic and operating platform. The types of information we collect include:

A. Account & Profile Information

When you register for an account, request a diagnostic report, or communicate with us, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, work email address, phone number, and physical address.
  • Agency Information: Agency name, website URL, team size, primary service offerings, revenue tier, and owner archetype responses.
  • Authentication Credentials: Password hashes and OAuth token identifiers used for account access.

B. Diagnostic & Operational Metrics

During the Agency Diagnostic and platform use, we collect operational data regarding your agency performance across 11 key departments (Leadership, Marketing, Sales, Billing, Onboarding, Service Delivery, Client Success, Talent Acquisition, Finance, Communication, Systems, and SOPs).

C. Connected Integration Data

If you choose to authorize integrations with third-party software (such as QuickBooks, FreshBooks, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, ClickUp, Monday.com, Teamwork, Slack, Google Drive, or Google Chat), we receive read-only or scoped API metadata necessary to compute scorecard KPIs and health indicators.

03. How We Use Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate business and platform operational purposes, including:

  • Generating Diagnostic Reports: Processing assessment answers to calculate your Owner Archetype, Independence Index, Agency Strength Index, and 14-point risk evaluation.
  • Operating the Platform: Powering your agency workspace dashboard, goal cascades, weekly L10 meeting cadence, and department performance metrics.
  • Customer Support & Executive Advising: Enabling assigned fractional advisors (such as your Fractional COO) to review evidence and conduct 1:1 strategy review sessions.
  • Service Improvements: Aggregating anonymized benchmark metrics to benchmark agency performance standards across industries.
  • System Notifications: Sending transactional updates, reset password links, diagnostic activation confirmations, and security alerts.

04. Third-Party Software & APIs

Creative Creatures offers optional back-office software integrations to automatically surface live agency KPIs:

  • OAuth Authorization: You explicitly authorize connection with third-party platforms via secure OAuth protocol. You may disconnect integrations at any time from your account settings.
  • Privacy Boundaries: For tools like Google Chat or Slack, only workspace and channel metadata required for team communication tracking is fetched. Message body content is not parsed or stored.
  • Third-Party Privacy Policies: Data processed by connected third-party providers remains subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

05. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your agency data except under the following limited circumstances:

  • Authorized Service Providers: Trusted vendors who provide infrastructure, hosting (Vercel, Supabase), billing processing, and email delivery, bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: If required by court order, legal process, law enforcement request, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of Creative Creatures or our users.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, where user data remains subject to pre-existing privacy commitments.

06. Security & Data Protection

We employ enterprise-grade security protocols to protect your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All web traffic is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.3. Sensitive parameters, auth tokens, and financial evidence data are encrypted using industry-standard AES encryption.
  • Access Controls: Multi-factor authentication, role-based access restrictions, and isolated database schemas.
  • Regular Audits: Continuous vulnerability scans and periodic security audits of our API handlers and infrastructure.

07. Data Retention & Storage

We retain your account and diagnostic data for as long as your workspace remains active or as needed to provide our services. You may request full account deletion or export of your diagnostic data at any time by contacting our support team.

08. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have specific rights regarding your personal and agency data:

  • Access & Export: Request a full copy of your diagnostic results and profile information.
  • Correction: Update or amend inaccurate account information through your profile dashboard.
  • Deletion: Request permanent removal of your account and diagnostic records from active servers.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from non-essential promotional communications via the link in any email footer.

09. Cookies & Tracking

Creative Creatures uses essential session cookies and local storage items (`localStorage`) strictly to maintain user sign-in state, diagnostic questionnaire progress, and theme preferences. We do not use intrusive third-party cross-site tracking cookies.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our platform or regulatory changes. When updates occur, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and notify active account holders via email or in-app notice.
